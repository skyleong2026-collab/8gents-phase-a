The Hollow Span

You are the Warden. The ravine is bigger than you can see, hidden by fog. Blightspawn roam it and guard the rich pockets — they'll hunt you. No timer; your enemy is what's out there, not a clock.

Your skills are weapons:
🗡 Jab click/F — fast strike, unlimited, your bread-and-butter · 💨 Dash Space — leap through enemies (brief invulnerability)
👊 Shove E — knock enemies back; shove them into the water to kill · ⛰ Rise Q — raise a wall to block them (and bridge water)

Scattered out there are 3 Tempest mods — find them across runs to assemble your ultimate build; each one permanently transforms a skill. Walk back to the start pad to extract & bank. Fall to 0 HP and you fall back — losing the run's satchel, never your build or bank.
