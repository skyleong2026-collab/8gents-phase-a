Grid-Net

Battle Network–style combat inside the fog-explore loop.

Walk into a red encounter → the battle opens on a Custom screen: the fight pauses. You see the board and a hand of 5 chips drawn from your folder — each card shows exactly which tiles it hits. Pick up to 3, press OK, and the fight resumes.

Now real-time: move tile-to-tile to dodge telegraphs, fire your loaded chips with Use Chip, and chip away with your free Buster. When the Custom gauge refills, hit Load Chips to pause and pick again.

Win to bank progress & collect new chips (Vulcan, Bolt, Bash, Tempest…) into your folder. Walk back to the start pad and press Enter to extract. Fall in battle: lose the run's progress, never your folder.
