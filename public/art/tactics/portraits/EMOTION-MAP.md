## TAINT RECIPE NOTE (learned 2026-06-15)
The strong taint look = "huge bright glowing violet-purple eyes radiating light, sickly grey-green
ashen skin, black veins, demonic, heavy purple+green glow" — and DROP "muted earth tones / no orange
tint" (that line fights the violet). Mask must sit ON the eyes; for hat-shadowed faces shift the mask
UP. Tom/Ruth/Eli taints were made with the OLD weak recipe (subtle violet) → re-roll for parity.
